# Pokémon Heartfelt Gold v1.0.15

## Downloads

- [Default patch](https://github.com/Trinsic64/Heartfelt-Gold/releases/download/heartfelt-v1.0.15/Heartfelt-Gold-v1.0.15-Patch.xdelta) for PC emulators with internal resolution upscaling.
- [Zoomed In patch](https://github.com/Trinsic64/Heartfelt-Gold/releases/download/heartfelt-v1.0.15/Heartfelt-Gold-v1.0.15-Patch-Zoomed-In.xdelta) for Nintendo DS hardware and emulators running at native resolution.
- [SHA-256 checksums](https://github.com/Trinsic64/Heartfelt-Gold/releases/download/heartfelt-v1.0.15/RELEASE-CHECKSUMS-HEARTFELT-v1.0.15.sha256).

## What changed

- Updated Pokémon move pools.
- Updated Pokémon abilities.
- Fixed evolution methods and targets.
- Restored Syrupy Apple to the upper 2F counter in Goldenrod Department Store, next to Tart Apple and Sweet Apple.
- Added dynamic cameras.

## Apply the patch

Apply one xdelta file directly to a clean, unmodified **Pokémon HeartGold (USA)** ROM with these hashes.

~~~~text
MD5      258cea3a62ac0d6eb04b5a0fd764d788
SHA-1    4fcded0e2713dc03929845de631d0932ea2b5a37
SHA-256  65f02a56842b75aa92d775d56d657a56fe3fa993550b04dc20704ab82d760105
~~~~

## Verification

Both patches reproduce the audited v31 builds byte for byte. Both builds passed boot and continue checks in DeSmuME with a copied save and differ only in Camera 0.

The [online player guide](https://trinsic64.github.io/Heartfelt-Gold/) documents v1.0.13.
